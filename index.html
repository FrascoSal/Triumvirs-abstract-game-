Triumvirs

Stack two commanders. Bring your army together.
How to play

Goal: move a Commander onto another friendly Commander to win. Simply placing them next to each other does not win. Jade moves first.

Your turn: move one Commander or Soldier one hex in any direction, or move a straight, unbroken line of 2–3 Soldiers together by up to 2–3 hexes respectively. Commanders move alone. In Along the line only mode, formations may only move forwards or backwards along their line; otherwise they may move in any of the six directions.

Movement: the whole formation must have a clear route at every step. No jumping or entering occupied hexes, except when joining two friendly Commanders. Soldiers in the formation may enter hexes that the same formation vacates.

Capture: after moving, remove each enemy caught directly between a moved piece and another friendly piece. Capture one enemy per sandwich, in any direction; all captures happen together. Board edges do not capture. Moving between two stationary enemies is safe. Commanders capture and can be captured just like Soldiers.

Draws: the game is drawn if both sides have at most one Commander, the same position with the same player to move occurs three times, or both players must pass. A player with no legal move passes automatically.

Controls: select a piece, or click 2–3 Soldiers in a straight line, then choose a numbered destination. Click a selected piece again to deselect it. Clear selection cancels your choice. Undo turn also reverses the AI reply. Changing board size or formation movement starts a new game.
